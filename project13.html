Effect of Bottle Gourd (Lagenaria Siceraria) Seed Powder on Growth, Lipid Profile, Hematological, and Tissue Redox Parameters of Rabbit

Physiology & Redox Ongoing

Research Overview

This study investigates the physiological and nutritional impacts of bottle gourd seed powder supplementation in rabbit models. The research evaluates how this additive influences overall growth performance, lipid profile, systemic blood health, and oxidative stress indicators.

Methodology

This study employs a controlled in vivo experimental design, dividing rabbits into control and dose-dependent dietary treatment groups. Following a designated feeding duration, physical growth metrics are recorded, blood samples are assayed for lipid and hematological profiling, and harvested tissues are analyzed to assess redox balance and antioxidant status.

Key Findings

This study is ongoing.
