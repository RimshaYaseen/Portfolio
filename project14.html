Effects Of Pumpkin Seed Essential Oil on Reproductive Hormones and Histopathology of Testis and Ovaries of Rabbits

Reproductive Physiology Ongoing

Research Overview

This study investigates the physiological impact of pumpkin seed essential oil on the reproductive health of rabbit models. The research focuses on evaluating how the extract influences circulating reproductive hormone profiles and examining any resulting cellular or structural modifications in the testes and ovaries through detailed histopathological analysis.

Methodology

This study employs a controlled in vivo experimental design, dividing male and female rabbits into control and dose-dependent treatment groups. Following the administration of pumpkin seed essential oil over a set duration, reproductive hormones are quantified via ELISA, and harvested reproductive tissues undergo histopathological examination to assess cellular and structural changes.

Key Findings

This study is ongoing.
